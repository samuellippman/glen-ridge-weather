"Most likely" = the per-hour median across the 6 Open-Meteo model sources (GFS, ECMWF, ICON, UKMO, GEM, and Open-Meteo's own best-match blend) — the shaded band behind each line is the min–max spread across those same 6 models, so a wide band means the models disagree and a narrow band means they agree. NWS is intentionally excluded here since it only reports temperature, precipitation chance, and wind, not the full variable set.
